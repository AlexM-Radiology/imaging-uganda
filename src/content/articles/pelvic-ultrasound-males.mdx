Pelvic ultrasound is a practical imaging tool for assessing the lower urinary tract in older male patients. It can be used to evaluate the prostate, urinary bladder, and surrounding structures when bowel or urinary symptoms are present.

## Common Indications

Ultrasound may be requested for:

- Lower urinary tract symptoms
- Difficulty passing urine
- Suspected urinary retention
- Assessment of enlarged prostate
- Evaluation of bowel-related symptoms due to pelvic mass effect

## Typical Findings

Common findings include:

- Enlarged prostate gland
- Median lobe protrusion into the bladder base
- Residual urine in the bladder after voiding
- Changes related to chronic bladder outlet obstruction

## Clinical Importance

Pelvic ultrasound provides a non-invasive way to assess patients with suspected prostate enlargement or obstruction. It can help clinicians understand whether symptoms are related to the prostate, bladder, or other pelvic pathology.
